## English letter

**Subject:** Request for permission to use Mufti Ismail Menk’s Stories of the Prophets in Quran Quest (educational app for children)

Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh,

My name is [Your name]. I am building **Quran Quest**, an educational Qur’an and Islamic-learning app for children and families.

We would like to offer **Mufti Ismail Menk’s Qisas / Stories of the Prophets** as an optional learning path called **Islamic Stories → Qisas al-Anbiya**. The story text shown to children would be a short, Qur’an-based child version. The **human narration** would be yours, clearly attributed to **Mufti Ismail Menk**.

We are **not** asking to claim ownership of the recordings. We will **not** copy, host, or redistribute the files until we have your written permission.

We respectfully ask for written permission for Quran Quest to:

1. **Stream** your Stories of the Prophets recordings inside the app.
2. **Host** the required audio files on our audio storage/CDN, if you allow hosting (otherwise we will stream only from an authorised source you name).
3. Allow **children** to listen through the app.
4. Use the recordings for **educational Islamic learning**.
5. Use the recordings **internationally**.
6. Use them on **Android**.
7. Use them on **iOS**.
8. Use them on **Web**.
9. Use **background playback** (audio continues if the child leaves the screen).
10. Use **lock-screen playback** (play, pause, and progress on the device lock screen).
11. **Display your name** as the narrator.
12. **Display source and attribution** as you specify.

Please also tell us:

- Whether **offline / cached playback** on the child’s device is permitted.
- Who the **copyright owner / rights holder** is.
- The **exact attribution** text we must show.
- The **authorised audio source** (files you provide, or an official URL we may use).
- Whether commercial / paid-app use is allowed later. If not, we will keep this path out of paid products as you instruct.

If permission is granted, we will:

- Attribute the narration clearly to Mufti Ismail Menk and the authorised source.
- Keep this audio **separate from Qur’an recitation**. They will never play at the same time.
- **Not** clone your voice or generate an AI imitation.
- Leave audio unpublished until your written permission is confirmed.

Jazakum Allahu khayran. We will wait for your written answer before using any recording.
