## English letter

**Subject:** Request for permission to stream Sheekh Maxamed Cabdi Umal’s Somali Tafsir in Quran Quest (educational app)

Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh,

My name is [Your name]. I am building **Quran Quest**, an educational Qur’an-learning app for children and families.

We would like to offer **Sheekh Maxamed Cabdi Umal’s Somali Tafsir** as an **optional** learning resource. It would sit beside the Arabic Qur’an and an already licensed Somali Qur’an meaning. The Tafsir would be clearly attributed to Sheikh Maxamed Cabdi Umal and to the authorised source/rightsholder you name.

We are **not** asking to claim ownership of the recordings. We will **not** copy the files into our app until we have your written permission.

We respectfully ask for written permission to:

1. **Stream** the authorised Tafsir audio inside the Quran Quest app.
2. **Store or cache** audio on the device only if technically necessary for playback (and only if you allow it). If you allow streaming only, we will not bundle or redistribute MP3 files.
3. Use the recordings for **educational** purposes for children and families.
4. Connect each recording to the relevant **surahs and ayahs** in a lesson.
5. Offer this on **Android, iOS, and Web**.
6. If applicable, include this optional Tafsir path in a **future paid/commercial** version of Quran Quest. If commercial use is not allowed, we will keep it out of paid products or disable it there, as you instruct.

Please also tell us:

- Who the **copyright owner / rights holder** is (Sheikh Umal, the mosque, a media office, or another organisation).
- Whether **Duruus Online** (https://www.duruusonline.com/tafsiirka-quraanka-sheekh-maxamed-cabdi-umal/) is an authorised distributor, or only a public host.
- The **exact attribution** text we must show.
- Whether we may stream only, or also cache/download for offline educational use.
- Whether commercial/paid-app use is allowed.
- The **authorised audio source** (official URL or files you provide), so we do not use unofficial copies.

If permission is granted, we will:

- Attribute the Tafsir clearly to Sheikh Maxamed Cabdi Umal and the authorised source.
- Keep Qur’an recitation and Tafsir as separate audio (they will never play at the same time).
- Make Tafsir optional. It will not start unless the family turns Tafsir Mode on.
- Not generate or invent Tafsir text. Audio would come only from the authorised recordings.

Jazakum Allahu khayran. We will wait for your written answer before using any audio.

Wassalamu alaikum,

## Somali letter

Assalaamu calaykum waraxmatullaahi wabarakaatuh,

Magacaygu waa [Magacaaga]. Waxaan dhisayaa **Quran Quest**, barnaamij waxbarasho Quraan oo loogu talagalay carruurta iyo qoysaska.

Waxaan jeclaan lahaa inaan ku darno **Tafsiirka Soomaaliga ee Sheekh Maxamed Cabdi Umal** sidii ilo **ikhtiyaari ah**. Wuxuu ka hoos imanayaa Quraanka Carabiga iyo macnaha Soomaaliga ee hore loo fasaxay. Tafsiirka si cad ayaa loogu tilmaami doonaa Sheekh Maxamed Cabdi Umal iyo isha/milkiilaha aad oggolaataan.

Si ixtiraam leh waxaan weydiisanaynaa oggolaansho qoraal ah:

1. In **codka tafsiirka** lagu **dhegeysto (stream)** gudaha barnaamijka Quran Quest.
2. In codka **lagu kaydiyo / cache** gudaha qalabka kaliya haddii farsamo ahaan loo baahdo (haddii aad oggolaataan). Haddii aad oggolaataan streaming keliya, MP3 ma gelin doono barnaamijka.
3. In loo isticmaalo **ujeeddo waxbarasho** carruur iyo qoysas.
4. In cashar kasta lala xiro **suuradaha iyo aayadaha** ku habboon.
5. In la isticmaalo **Android, iOS, iyo Web**.
6. Haddii khusayso, in habkan ikhtiyaariga ah lagu daro **nooc mustaqbal ah oo lacag lagu bixiyo**. Haddii isticmaalka ganacsi aan la oggolayn, waan ka fogaanaynaa sidii aad faraysaan.

- Yaa ah **milkiilaha xuquuqda**.
- In **Duruus Online** ay tahay qaybiyaha la oggol yahay, mise kaliya martigeliye dadweyne.
- **Qoraalka tilmaanta (attribution)** ee aan muujin karno.
- In streaming keliya la oggol yahay, mise sidoo kale kaydin offline.
- In isticmaalka ganacsi/lacag la bixiyo la oggol yahay.
- **Isha rasmiga ah** ee cajaladaha.

Haddii la oggolaado, tafsiirku wuxuu ahaanayaa ikhtiyaari; ma bilaabayo ilaa qoysku furo Habka Tafsiirka. Quraanka iyo tafsiirku marna isku mar ma ciyaari doonaan. Ma samayn doono qoraal tafsiir ah oo AI been abuuray.

Jazaakumullaahu khayran.
