| Original recording owner / rightsholder | **Not confirmed.** The spoken work is by **Sheekh Maxamed Cabdi Umal**. Copyright in a recorded lecture typically sits with the speaker and/or the person/organisation that made the recording (often a mosque or media team). Public reporting identifies Sheikh Umal as imam of **Masjid Abubakar (6th Street Mosque), Eastleigh, Nairobi**. That is the most plausible rights-holder route. It is not proof of ownership. |

The same Umal tafsir set appears on other sites (for example Ceegaag) and in unofficial apps. Hosting a download page is not a copyright grant and is not a sublicense for a third-party app. |
| Does Duruus Online have permission to distribute it? | **Unknown.** No public licence, rights notice, or sublicense text was found on the tafsir page, the [contact form](https://www.duruusonline.com/contact/), or the [privacy policy](https://www.duruusonline.com/privacy-policy/). The privacy policy covers personal data (GDPR) and is not a content licence. The terms page linked from that policy was not available (`/terms-and-conditions/` returned 404). |

| Required attribution | **Unknown.** Until the rights holder specifies wording, planned attribution is: *Tafsiir — Sheekh Maxamed Cabdi Umal*, plus the authorised source they name. |

Ask Duruus Online only whether they own the rights or can introduce the owner. A Duruus “yes, you may hotlink our MP3s” is not enough unless they prove they can sublicense. |
