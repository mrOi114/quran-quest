## What Quran Quest will not do until permission is written

- Download YouTube audio
- Rip audio from websites
- Copy another app’s MP3 files
- Upload Sheikh recordings to our storage/CDN
- Clone a Sheikh’s voice
- Generate an AI imitation of a Sheikh
- Treat “free to listen” as “free to redistribute”
